Cards & Collection
1) !cards
   - Drops 3 random player cards (image compiled from 3 PlayerCard entries).
   - You must claim one card by reacting to the drop message:
     - 1️⃣ = first card
     - 2️⃣ = second card
     - 3️⃣ = third card

2) !collection
   - Shows your card collection with pagination.
   - Pagination controls (on the collection message):
     - ◀️ previous page
     - ▶️ next page

3) !stats [player_name]
   - If `player_name` is provided: shows stats for the matching PlayerCard in the catalog.
   - If omitted: shows stats for your most recently claimed card.

Teams 
4) !create_team <team_name> <instance_id_1> <instance_id_2> <instance_id_3> <instance_id_4> <instance_id_5> <instance_id_6>
   - Creates your team with 6 slots.
   - Instance IDs are assigned in this order:
     - 1: point_guard
     - 2: shooting_guard
     - 3: small_forward
     - 4: power_forward
     - 5: center
     - 6: sixth_man

5) !rename_team <new_team_name>
   - Renames your team.

6) !update_team
   - Starts an interactive team update:
     - React with a position:
       1️⃣ point_guard
       2️⃣ shooting_guard
       3️⃣ small_forward
       4️⃣ power_forward
       5️⃣ center
       6️⃣ sixth_man
     - Then send the desired player `instance_id` as the next message.

7) !view_team
   - Displays your team (all 6 slots) as text.

Trading & Giving
8) !trade <discord_id> <offer_items...>
   - Initiates a trade offer to the target Discord user.
   - `discord_id` must be the numeric ID of the target user.
   - `offer_items...` can include:
     - Card instance IDs (the alphanumeric `instance_id` from your collection)
     - Optional cash token (starts with `$`, e.g. `$100`)
   - Flow:
     1. Target user reacts ✅/❌ to accept/decline.
     2. If accepted, the target must reply with:
        !return <return_items...>
     3. Then a final confirmation happens (✅/❌) between both players.

9) !return <return_items...>
   - Used ONLY as a reply during a trade flow (see `!trade` step 2).
   - Same item format as trade offers:
     - Card instance IDs and/or a `$<amount>` cash token.

10) !giveaway @target_user <give_items...>
   - Gives cards/cash to another user.
   - `give_items...` format:
     - Card instance IDs and/or a `$<amount>` cash token.
   - Flow:
     1. The target user reacts ✅/❌ to accept/decline.
     2. If accepted, ownership/court cash is transferred.

Gameplay (Challenge / Match)
11) !challenge @User
   - Starts a match between two teams.
   - Flow:
     1. Target team reacts ✅/❌ to accept/decline the challenge.
     2. If accepted, both teams go into an optional wager stage (wagers are currently not fully functional).
     3. Then the game simulation runs (quarter-by-quarter prompts).

12) !wager <wager_text>
   - Used ONLY during the wager stage after a challenge is accepted.
   - This bot currently stores wager messages as raw text (it does not strictly parse the format).
   - The in-game prompt suggests including court cash and instance IDs; example:
     - !wager $100 instance_id_1 instance_id_2

Reactions used internally (for reference)
- Card drop claim:
  - 1️⃣ / 2️⃣ / 3️⃣
- Collection pagination:
  - ◀️ / ▶️
- Team update position selection:
  - 1️⃣-6️⃣
- Trade / giveaway confirmations:
  - ✅ / ❌
- Challenge acceptance:
  - ✅ / ❌
